# Sales-Performance-Analysis-PowerBI
Interactive Power BI dashboard for sales performance and profitability analysis

## Project Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze sales performance, profitability, product trends, and regional performance.

The project demonstrates an end-to-end business intelligence workflow, from data preparation and modeling to DAX calculations, dashboard development, and business insights.

## Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- Power Query
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Data Modeling (Star Schema)
- Data Visualization
- Business Intelligence

## Dataset

This project uses a synthetic sales dataset containing 50 records across four regions and three product categories.

The dataset includes order dates, products, categories, regions, quantities, unit prices, and unit costs.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Sales Overview

- Total Sales, Total Profit, and Profit Margin KPIs
- Total Orders and Average Order Value
- Monthly Sales Trends
- Sales by Product and Region
- Interactive Slicers

### 2. Profitability Analysis

- Total Profit, Total Cost, and Profit Margin
- Profit by Product
- Profit Margin by Product
- Profit by Region
- Sales vs. Profit by Category
- Key Business Insights

## Key Findings

- Total Sales: 27,254.50
- Total Profit: 10,162.50
- Profit Margin: 37.29%
- Office Chair generated the highest total profit.
- Notebook achieved the highest product profit margin.
- Jeddah recorded the highest regional profit.

## Business Recommendations

- Monitor high-revenue products alongside their profit margins.
- Investigate opportunities to increase sales of high-margin products.
- Analyze regional profitability differences.
- Use interactive reporting to support business performance monitoring.

1. Download the Power BI report file.
